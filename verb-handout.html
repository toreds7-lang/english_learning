Verbals infinitives · gerunds · participles

Infinitives

  • to + V1 after many verbs and adjectives: I want to learn. It’s easy to use.
  • Purpose, to + V1 = in order to: She went out to buy milk.
  • No to after modals and make / let + object: They made me wait.
  • to have + V3 · to be + V3: She seems to have forgotten.

Gerunds (-ing as a noun)

  • As the subject: Swimming is good exercise.
  • After prepositions: good at drawing · thanks for coming
  • go + V-ing for activities: Let’s go swimming.

Participles

  • -ing adjective = causes the feeling; -ed = has the feeling: The lesson was boring, so I was bored.
  • Participle clauses: Having finished dinner, we went for a walk.

Regular & irregular

  • Regular: V1 + -ed, V2 = V3: play – played – played
  • Irregular patterns: A-A-A cut–cut–cut · A-B-B buy–bought–bought · A-B-C sing–sang–sung · A-B-A come–came–come
  • -ed sounds: /t/ walked · /d/ played · /ɪd/ wanted

Agreement & helping verbs

  • he / she / it → V-s: My brother lives in Leeds. My brothers live in Leeds.
  • be · do · have build tenses, questions, negatives, the passive: Do you like it? She has left.

Verb types

  • Transitive + object: She opened the door. Intransitive: The baby is sleeping.
  • Two objects: She gave me a book. Linking + adjective: The soup smells good.
  • State verbs (know, like, want, own, seem) are not usually continuous: I know. not I’m knowing.
  • Phrasal verbs: Turn it off. · look after her · The plane took off. · put up with

  • Causative have / get + object + V3: I had my hair cut.
  • Reporting: She is said to be in London.

Mood

  • Imperative: Close the door. Don’t be late. Let’s go.
  • wish + past (now) · wish + had V3 (past): I wish I had more time.
  • If I were you, I’d call her. It’s time we left.

Conditionals

Zero always true
if + present, present
If you heat ice, it melts.
First real future
if + present, will + V1
If it rains, we’ll stay home.
Second unreal now
if + past, would + V1
If I had a car, I’d drive to work.
Third unreal past
if + had V3, would have V3
If we’d left earlier, we wouldn’t have missed it.
Mixed past → now
if + had V3, would + V1
If I’d studied medicine, I’d be a doctor now.

Tenses & Aspects time × aspect = 12 tenses

  • Future forms: be going to plans, evidence (It’s going to rain.) · present continuous arrangements (I’m meeting Sam at six.) · present simple timetables (The train leaves at 7:15.)
  • used to + V1 past habits: I used to play the violin.
  • Timelines: dashed = now · dot = complete action · wave = in progress · arrow = up to a point
  • Signal words: ago, yesterday → past simple · now, at the moment → present continuous · ever, never, just, yet, since, for → present perfect
  • Reported speech moves one step back: is → was has done → had done will → would

Two ways to speak English

영어로 말하는 두 가지 방식: 피아노 모드와 게임 모드

She coached thousands of people in Southeast Asia for 20 years and found a surprise: the best communicators did not always have the best English.

Piano mode 피아노 모드
Game mode 게임 모드
Focus 집중
me and my mistakes
the other person and the goal
Score 점수

Feeling 기분

calm, even fun
Result 결과
freeze, talk in circles

Her three questions

  1. What makes people like Faizal different? They speak in game mode.
  2. Why does it matter? English is now a tool, used mostly between non-native speakers.
  3. What can you do today? Focus on the other person and the result you want.

Idea 1 of 50:22 – 1:47

Attitude beats level

소통을 잘하는 것은 영어 레벨보다 태도에 달려 있다

“How well somebody communicates in English actually has very little to do with their English level. It has a lot to do with their attitude.”Marianna Pascal, 0:38
  1. Faizal, a factory supervisor. His English level was very low, but he listened calmly and then answered clearly. He expressed his thoughts “beautifully”. 영어 수준은 낮았지만, 차분히 듣고 분명하게 답했다
  2. So the goal is not perfect English. The goal is calm, clear communication, and you can do that at any level. 목표는 '완벽한 영어'가 아니라 '차분하고 분명한 소통'

At work

Think of a coworker whose English is simple but who always gets things done in English meetings. That person is a Faizal.

영어는 단순해도 회의에서 늘 일을 해내는 동료가 바로 '파이잘'이다

Idea 2 of 51:53 – 4:03

Piano mode: counting your mistakes

피아노 모드: 실수를 세며 말하는 습관

Her daughter hated piano lessons, because “her success in piano was measured by how few mistakes she made.” Many people go into English conversations with the same dread.2:51
  1. Score = mistakes. You walk in afraid of being judged, so you play it safe or stay quiet. 점수 = 실수 개수 → 평가받을까 두려워 말을 아낀다
  2. A bad self-image. “Good English sounds like that. Mine is far away. I'm a bad English speaker.” '좋은 영어'와 내 영어를 비교하며 '나는 영어 못하는 사람'이라고 믿는다
  3. It shows when you talk. “My English not so good, lah. Cannot. Sorry.” (lah is a Malaysian English word at the end of a sentence.)

The Korean office version

Opening with “Sorry, my English is not good…” makes the listener worry before you've said anything. Fix: skip the apology and start with your point.

“제 영어가 부족해서…”로 시작하지 말고, 바로 요점부터 말하자

Wordsdread 두려움screw up 망치다, 실수하다judge 평가하다

Idea 3 of 54:16 – 6:19

Game mode: eyes on the target

게임 모드: 실수가 아니라 목표를 본다

In a cyber café she watched a terrible gamer with three friends behind him. “There was no embarrassment. There was no feeling of being judged. There was no shyness.” Just focus, and a smile.5:24
  1. He wasn't good, and he didn't care. All he thought about was the goal: the bad guys on the screen. 실력은 별로였지만 신경 쓰지 않았다. 오직 목표만 봤다
  2. This is how Faizal speaks. He is “entirely focused on the person he's speaking to and the result he wants to get.” 대화 상대와 원하는 결과에만 집중한다
  3. No self-watching. “No self-awareness, no thoughts about his own mistakes.”

Think like a gamer

In a game, a miss is not a failure: you just shoot again. In English, a wrong word is not a failure: you just say it another way.

게임에서 빗나가면 다시 쏘듯, 말이 안 통하면 다른 말로 다시 하면 된다

Idea 4 of 59:10 – 10:14

English is a tool, not an art

영어는 완성해야 할 예술이 아니라, 결과를 얻기 위한 도구다

1 : 5native : non-native speakers
96%of English talks include a non-native speaker
4%are native ↔ native only
“This is not my language anymore. This language belongs to you. It's not an art to be mastered; it's just a tool to use to get a result.”10:03
  1. Your listeners are mostly people like you. Clients and partners in Vietnam, Germany or India want clear English, not perfect English. 거래처·파트너 대부분도 비원어민이다. 완벽함보다 명확함을 원한다
  2. A tool is judged by what it does. Did the meeting decide something? Did the customer get the part? That is the score. 도구는 결과로 평가한다: 결정이 났는가? 고객이 부품을 받았는가?

Her numbers are from 2017. Other estimates (for example Ethnologue) are closer to 1 native speaker for every 3 non-native speakers, but the point is the same: most English is spoken by non-native speakers. 수치는 출처마다 다르지만, 영어 사용자 대부분이 비원어민이라는 점은 같다.

Idea 5 of 512:01 – 14:42

Why we freeze: correct vs. clear

우리가 얼어붙는 이유: '정확하게'와 '분명하게'를 동시에 하려 해서

At school, students “are judged more on correctness than on clarity.” One student clearly understood the text but got a big X for writing “enviroment”.12:14

We bring the school habit to work. In a stressful talk you try to get a result and say it perfectly. “Your brain … cannot do two things at once,” so it shuts down. 결과도 내고 문법도 완벽하게: 두 가지를 동시에 하려다 머리가 멈춘다

Three signs your brain is shutting down

1Listening goes. You're planning your answer, so you don't hear what they said. 듣기가 사라진다
2Speaking goes. Words you know just disappear. 아는 단어도 안 나온다
3Confidence goes. Worse, the other person may think you can't do the job. 자신감이 사라지고, 상대는 업무 능력 부족으로 오해한다

What to do, starting today

“When you speak, don't focus on yourself. Focus on the other person and the result you want to achieve.”

말할 때 나 자신에게 집중하지 말고, 상대방과 내가 얻고 싶은 결과에 집중하라.

Who am I talking to?

Every practice slide has a timer and a recorder. Say your answer out loud first, then tap a card to compare.

Why these methods · beyond the talk

  • Game mode still needs a plan. Gamers know the mission before they press Start. A 10-second plan frees your brain during the talk. 미리 10초 계획하면 대화 중 머리가 가벼워진다
  • Fluency grows from repetition under time pressure. The 60-45-30 retell is based on Paul Nation's 4/3/2 activity. 같은 내용을 시간을 줄여 반복하면 유창성이 는다
  • Accuracy still matters, in the right place. Check grammar in emails and documents, and review after a conversation, not in the middle of it. 정확성은 글쓰기와 사후 복습에서 챙기자
  • Short is not rude. Keep “please”, “could you”, “thanks”. Drop the 20 extra words. 짧아도 공손할 수 있다. 공손한 한두 단어만 남기기
  • Rhythm helps you speak smoothly. Don't stop to fix every mistake. Finish the sentence, then fix it if you need to. 틀려도 문장을 끝까지 말하고, 필요하면 그다음에 고치기

Practice 5 of 6For the 3 freeze signs

Rescue phrases: when your brain freezes

머리가 하얘질 때 꺼내 쓰는 구조 표현

1 · Listening goes 못 알아들었을 때

  • Sorry, could you say that again?
  • Could you speak a bit slower, please?
  • Do you mean …?
  • So you need … by …, right?

2 · Speaking goes 말이 안 나올 때

  • Give me a second.
  • How can I say this…
  • Let me say it another way.
  • It's a kind of …
  • Can I show you on the screen?

3 · Confidence goes 자신감이 떨어질 때

  • Good question. Let me think.
  • Let me check I understood: …
  • I'll check and email you today.
  • Let me finish my point.

Which phrase? Say it, then tap

Learn 2 phrases from each group by heart. When your brain freezes, there's no time to build a sentence, so have them ready.

Practice 6 of 6Fluency builder

Say it three times: 60, 45, 30 seconds

같은 이야기를 60초 → 45초 → 30초로 세 번 말하기

  1. Round 1 · 60 s. Tell the whole story. Don't stop for mistakes. 실수해도 멈추지 말고 끝까지
  2. Round 2 · 45 s. Same story, shorter. Cut the extra details. 같은 이야기를 더 짧게
  3. Round 3 · 30 s. Only the key points. Record this round and listen. 핵심만. 이 라운드를 녹음해서 들어 보기

Pick a story

The pharmacy storyThe barbecue storyYour work this weekA problem you solvedYour job, for a new coworker

Why it works

By round 3 you already know what to say, so your brain has room for how to say it: speed and smoothness improve. This is Paul Nation's 4/3/2 activity, made shorter for a work break.
